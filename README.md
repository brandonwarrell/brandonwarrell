# Hi, I'm Brandon Warrell

I'm a graphic designer in Palmetto, Florida, in the Sarasota–Bradenton area. My legal name is William Brandon Warrell, and I go by Brandon.

I design posters, packaging, vehicle wraps, logos and social graphics, and I prepare print-ready files. I studied graphic design at State College of Florida, Manatee–Sarasota, where I earned the Graphic Design Support certificate.

This GitHub is for the other side of what I make: 3D models and parts that have to fit real things.

## What's here

- **[studio-suite-cadquery](https://github.com/brandonwarrell/studio-suite-cadquery)**: a 15' × 21' room addition modeled in Python with CadQuery. One config file in feet drives the 3D model, a DXF floor plan, and exports for STEP, STL and Unreal Engine 5.
- **[3d-printed-parts](https://github.com/brandonwarrell/3d-printed-parts)**: parametric Python generators for parts I measured with calipers and designed to fit things in my house. A tub drain plug, a power-strip dock for a desk leg, and corner brackets for a plywood dog staircase.

## Tools I use

- **Design:** Adobe Illustrator, Photoshop, InDesign, After Effects, Premiere Pro, Lightroom, Canva
- **3D:** Blender, CadQuery (Python), trimesh, RealityScan photogrammetry
- **Making:** Flashforge Adventurer 3 printer (ABS and PLA), digital calipers
